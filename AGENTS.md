# Working rules

- Use `py -3.10` for this project's Python environment.
- Prioritize falsifiable, evidence-backed fixes to control resolution, available
  information, objectives, data, and learning structure. Hyperparameter tuning
  in general is **not** the default first intervention. Tune parameters when
  evidence specifically points to optimization or their setting as the bottleneck.
- Change one mechanism at a time. Declare mechanical unit conversions separately
  from independent tuning; preserve a byte-identical checkpoint baseline and
  record what changed in Git and experiment metadata.
- Shaped return, entropy, explained variance, movement, kills and damage are not
  verified competence. Use held-out full-floor outcomes and native evidence.
- Preserve live training unless the user authorizes a stop or handover. A specific
  handover approval is not an automatic restart policy. Do not touch unrelated
  Steam processes or personal saves. Keep the requested six-worker configuration.
- Consult the current section of OPERATIONS.md before interacting with native
  processes; older sections are historical.
